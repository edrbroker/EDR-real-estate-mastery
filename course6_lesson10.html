Closing the Deal — Final Negotiation Strategy

From counter-offer resolution to closed escrow — the last mile of the transaction
~10 min readRPA ¶14G / ¶16 / ¶17 / ¶295 Quiz Questions
📋 Setting the Scene

The Okonkwos and the sellers of 5555 Winners Lane have agreed: $1,238,000, 25-day close, $12,000 seller credit, buyer keeps all contingency periods. All that stands between now and closing is the final stretch — and that's where deals still fall apart.

Great negotiators don't relax at acceptance. They stay focused through closing day.

What Happens After Acceptance

The negotiation doesn't end when the offer is accepted — it continues through every phase of the transaction. Here's where experienced agents earn their fee:

The Final Walkthrough — RPA ¶14G

The final walkthrough (also called final verification of condition) takes place 5 days before the close of escrow under RPA ¶14G. It is NOT a contingency — it's a right to verify the property is in the same condition as acceptance, with agreed-upon repairs complete.

📌 What to Check at Final Walkthrough

All agreed-upon repairs are completed and receipted. Seller has removed all personal property. The property is in the same condition as when the offer was accepted. Appliances and included items are present and working. Smoke detectors, CO detectors in place.

When the Appraisal Comes In Low

If the appraisal comes in below the purchase price, buyers have options under the appraisal contingency (RPA ¶14L(2)):

  • Negotiate a price reduction: Seller reduces to the appraised value
  • Appraisal gap coverage: Buyer covers the difference between appraisal and purchase price in cash
  • Hybrid: Seller reduces partially; buyer covers the rest
  • Cancel: Buyer cancels under the appraisal contingency and recovers EMD
⚠️ Wire Fraud — The Closing Day Threat

California real estate transactions are a primary target for wire fraud. Criminals intercept email communications and send fake wire instructions. Always verify wire transfer instructions by calling the escrow officer DIRECTLY at a number you looked up independently — not a number from an email. This one habit has saved buyers from losing hundreds of thousands of dollars.

✅ The Closing Conversation

On closing day, call your clients. Walk them through what to expect. Confirm they've received and verified wire instructions directly. Tell them what time to expect the keys. Closing day should feel celebratory — and your presence and guidance make that possible. This is why clients refer you to their friends.

RPA References: ¶14G — Final Verification of Condition (5 days before COE). ¶16 — Possession and keys. ¶17 — Prorations (30-day month basis). ¶29 — Liquidated damages (3% cap, 1–4 unit owner-occupied).
