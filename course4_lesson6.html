Closing Costs — Who Pays What

Breaking down buyer and seller closing costs so you can set accurate expectations from day one
~10 min readRPA ¶3 / ¶17 / Local Custom5 Quiz Questions
📋 The Sticker Shock

Marcus calls you. He just saw the estimated closing statement from escrow. "Wait — I thought the down payment was the only thing we were paying? What are all these other fees?" This is a conversation every agent has, and you need to be ready for it.

Understanding Closing Costs

Closing costs are fees paid at the close of escrow that are separate from the down payment. They typically range from 2% to 5% of the purchase price for buyers and 6% to 8% for sellers (largely due to agent commissions).

Typical Buyer Closing Costs

  • Loan origination fee — lender's fee for processing the loan (typically 0.5%–1% of loan)
  • Appraisal fee — $500–$900, ordered by the lender
  • Credit report fee — typically $30–$75
  • Title insurance — lender's policy (ALTA) — buyer typically pays
  • Escrow fee — buyer's share (split 50/50 with seller in most CA counties)
  • Recording fees — county charges to record the deed and deed of trust
  • Prepaid interest — interest from close of escrow to first mortgage payment
  • Property tax impound — lender collects several months upfront into an impound account
  • Homeowner's insurance — first year's premium paid at close

Typical Seller Closing Costs

  • Real estate commissions — the largest seller cost
  • Owner's title insurance policy (CLTA) — seller customarily pays in SoCal
  • Escrow fee — seller's share (split with buyer)
  • County transfer tax — $1.10 per $1,000 of sales price in most CA counties
  • Payoff of existing mortgage — plus any prepayment penalty
  • HOA transfer fees and document fees — if applicable
  • Natural Hazard Disclosure (NHD) report — typically seller pays
  • Home warranty — if offered (RPA ¶18 — seller max contribution is $625)
RPA ¶17: Prorations (property taxes, HOA, etc.) are calculated on a 30-day month basis as of the close of escrow date. The escrow officer handles these calculations on the closing/settlement statement.
✓ Set Expectations Early

On a $1,250,000 purchase like 5555 Winners Lane, buyer closing costs could easily be $25,000–$50,000 on top of the down payment. Buyers who don't know this upfront often panic when they see the closing statement. Walk them through estimated costs during your very first buyer consultation.
