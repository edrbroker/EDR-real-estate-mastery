Full Disclosure Scenario — 5555 Winners Lane

Bringing it all together — a complete disclosure walkthrough for the Okonkwo transaction
~10 min readCivil Code §1102 / §1103 / §2079 / RPA ¶14A5 Quiz Questions
📋 Setting the Scene

The Okonkwos are 7 days into escrow on 5555 Winners Lane. The disclosure package arrives from the listing agent. As the buyer's agent, this is your moment. Everything you've learned in this course comes into play right now.

The Full Disclosure Package — What Arrives

In a standard California residential transaction, the buyer's agent should receive — and review with their clients — the following documents:

DocumentRequired ByTimeline
Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS)Civil Code §1102Within 7 days of acceptance
Seller Property Questionnaire (SPQ)C.A.R. Standard PracticeWith or shortly after TDS
Natural Hazard Disclosure (NHD)Civil Code §1103Before close of escrow
Agent Visual Inspection Disclosure (AVID)Civil Code §2079Both agents — as soon as practical
Lead-Based Paint DisclosureFederal CFR 40, Part 745Before offer (pre-1978 only)
HOA Documents PackageCivil Code §1368 / RPA ¶N(3)Within 3 days of acceptance; seller orders
Agency Disclosure (C.A.R. Form AD)Civil Code §2079.14Before or when offer is prepared
Mello-Roos DisclosureGov. Code §53340.2Typically included in NHD report

Walking Through 5555 Winners Lane

Here's what you find when you review the disclosure package for the Okonkwos:

  • TDS: Seller checked "yes" to a prior roof repair — but left the explanation blank. Action: Request repair documentation and permit records before your clients sign off.
  • SPQ: Seller disclosed a prior homeowner's insurance claim for water intrusion in the rear bedroom 4 years ago. Action: Request claim documentation and verify complete repair.
  • NHD: Property is not in a flood zone, but IS in a Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone. Action: Immediately advise clients to confirm insurance availability before removing the insurance contingency.
  • Lead Disclosure: Property was built in 1968. Lead disclosure is required. Seller has signed it; you verify the EPA pamphlet was delivered. Complete.
  • HOA: 5555 Winners Lane is in an HOA. Reserve fund study shows 34% funded — borderline but acceptable. Monthly dues are $425. No pending litigation or special assessments. Action: Explain to clients; no immediate red flags.
  • AVID: Listing agent's AVID notes the popcorn ceiling. Your own AVID (from your walkthrough) notes the popcorn ceiling and the water stain in the rear bedroom. Documented and consistent with SPQ claim.
✅ Your Value as an Agent

This is exactly where a knowledgeable buyer's agent earns their fee. Reviewing these documents isn't paperwork — it's protection. The Okonkwos are making the largest purchase of their lives. Your job is to make sure they know exactly what they're buying.

Disclosure and Contingency Timing

📌 Key Connection

All major contingencies — inspection, loan, appraisal, insurance, seller documents — are designed to give buyers time to review these disclosures and cancel if needed. Never let your clients remove contingencies before they've thoroughly reviewed the full disclosure package.
